# Figure legends — HI working version, 2026-10-18

The following five legends are reproduced from the author-approved Integrated manuscript without rewriting. Supplementary figure legends are included in [Online Resource 1](ESM_1.pdf).

Fig. 1 Selection and allocation of the Chinese cohort. Of 2,538 screened patients, 2,093 were excluded and 445 underwent outcome-stratified 70:30 allocation to development (312) and internal validation (133). Categories have no separate counts. Deaths before target readmission were excluded as described in Materials and Methods. ACLF, acute-on-chronic liver failure; HBV, hepatitis B virus

Fig. 2 Complementary feature selection and identification of Core4. (a) Boruta selection; albumin (†) was initially tentative and retained after TentativeRoughFix. (b) All 49 LASSO coefficient paths; the five terms with nonzero coefficients at λ₁se are highlighted in purple, and the others are gray. (c) Tenfold cross-validation, with error bars showing ±1 standard error. λmin and λ₁se are marked in b and c. (d) The intersection of LASSO and Boruta selections comprised INR, PLT, ALBI, and FIB-4 (Core4). HBeAg status represents the joint HBeAg/anti-HBe category. The selected categorical HBV DNA term contrasts ≥20,000 with <2,000 IU/mL. λ denotes the LASSO penalty parameter. Abbreviations: ALBI, albumin-bilirubin score; ALP, alkaline phosphatase; ALT, alanine aminotransferase; anti-HBe, antibody to hepatitis B e antigen; APRI, aspartate aminotransferase-to-platelet ratio index; AST, aspartate aminotransferase; BMI, body mass index; DNA, deoxyribonucleic acid; EGVH, esophagogastric variceal hemorrhage; FIB-4, fibrosis-4 index; GGT, gamma-glutamyl transferase; Hb, hemoglobin; HBeAg, hepatitis B e antigen; HBsAg, hepatitis B surface antigen; HBV, hepatitis B virus; HRS, hepatorenal syndrome; INR, international normalized ratio; LASSO, least absolute shrinkage and selection operator; LDH, lactate dehydrogenase; LMR, lymphocyte-to-monocyte ratio; LYM, lymphocyte count; MON, monocyte count; NAR, neutrophil-to-albumin ratio (10 × NEU/albumin, with NEU in 10⁹/L and albumin in g/L); NEU, neutrophil count; NLR, neutrophil-to-lymphocyte ratio; PLR, platelet-to-lymphocyte ratio; PLT, platelet count; PMR, platelet-to-monocyte ratio; PNI, prognostic nutritional index; PTAR, study-defined 10 × INR/albumin, with albumin in g/L; SBP, spontaneous bacterial peritonitis; Scr, serum creatinine; SIRI, systemic inflammation response index; TBIL, total bilirubin; WBC, white blood cell count.

Fig. 3 Performance of seven candidate models. (a, b) Internal receiver operating characteristic and precision–recall curves (133 patients; 55 events). (c, d) Nested performance across 95 valid LASSO and elastic-net evaluations and 98 for each other model; boxes, medians and interquartile ranges; whiskers, extreme values within 1.5 interquartile ranges; dots, overlapping partitions. (e, f) External curves (102 episodes; 50 patients; 67 event episodes). AUROC, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve; AP, average precision; LASSO, least absolute shrinkage and selection operator; RBF-SVM, radial-basis-function support vector machine; XGBoost, Extreme Gradient Boosting; LightGBM, Light Gradient Boosting Machine

Fig. 4 Benchmarking risk strata calibration and decision curves. (a) Paired RBF-SVM–MELD-Na contrasts, with 95% confidence intervals. Positive differences favor RBF-SVM; Brier improvement is benchmark minus RBF-SVM. Nested intervals condition on aggregated held-out predictions from the same 445 patients. (b) Risk distributions by outcome. (c) Development-derived strata; intervals are Wilson internally and cluster-bootstrap externally. (d, e) Calibration: five internal and four external quantile groups per model; fixed-group vertical bars are 5,000-resample percentile intervals. Curves are diagnostic logistic fits over observed probability ranges, without confidence bands or recalibration. (f, g) Decision curves over thresholds 0.10–0.60. Internal resampling uses patients; external resampling uses patient clusters. MELD-Na is a development-fitted single-predictor logistic benchmark; nested fits use corresponding training partitions. SVM denotes RBF-SVM. Core4 comprises INR, PLT, ALBI, and FIB-4. Definitions and full-range decision curves are in Online Resource 1, Note M4 and Fig. S2. MELD-Na, Model for End-Stage Liver Disease incorporating sodium; CITL, calibration-in-the-large; AUROC, area under the receiver operating characteristic curve; AP, average precision; RBF-SVM, radial-basis-function support vector machine; INR, international normalized ratio; PLT, platelet count; ALBI, albumin–bilirubin score; FIB-4, fibrosis-4 index

Fig. 5 Global and individual prediction attribution. (a, b) SHAP distributions internally (133 patients) and externally (102 episodes; 50 patients); shared predictor-specific 5th–95th percentile color limits retain all observations. (c–f) Dependence plots. (g, h) Two post hoc illustrative internal cases with similar MELD-Na scores. Exact Kernel SHAP decomposes probabilities using 312 locked development background records; E[f(X)] is baseline output and f(x) is individual prediction. PLT also enters FIB-4. Background and numerical checks are in Online Resource 1, Note M5. SHAP, SHapley Additive exPlanations; INR, international normalized ratio; PLT, platelet count; ALBI, albumin–bilirubin score; FIB-4, fibrosis-4 index; P5/P95, 5th/95th percentiles; MELD-Na, Model for End-Stage Liver Disease incorporating sodium; RBF-SVM, radial-basis-function support vector machine
